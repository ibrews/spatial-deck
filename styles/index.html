Spatial Deck looks

Each look is a complete art direction from a real printed or built tradition: tokens, type, a frame, one motion grammar and a motion-graphic palette. Pick one with ?look=<id>, with tools/apply_look.py --default <id>, or when starting a talk with tools/new_talk.sh --look <id>. Rules are in styles/README.md.

Atlas

atlas · rules

A survey map: buff paper, a neatline with a map collar, brown contours, blue water, letterspaced caps for names and italic for water features.

USGS 7.5-minute topographic quadrangle conventions (neatline, collar, scale bar, benchmarks); Swiss and British survey cartography.

Bauhaus

bauhaus · rules

Dessau-era printed matter: cream stock, black, the three primaries, a geometric sans set lowercase, circle / square / triangle as structure.

Bauhaus Dessau print shop (Herbert Bayer, László Moholy-Nagy, Joost Schmidt), 1925-32; Bayer's universal lowercase alphabet.

Broadside

broadside · rules

A letterpress broadside: newsprint stock, everything centered, a slab display face with a book serif, thick-thin rules and fleurons, one red second color.

19th-century American letterpress broadsides, handbills and auction notices set in wood and metal type.

Cyanotype

cyanotype · rules

Photogram plates: a Prussian blue ground, white where light was held back, plates numbered and captioned in italic.

Anna Atkins, Photographs of British Algae: Cyanotype Impressions (1843); the blueprint copies of engineering drawings that used the same process.

Riso

A risograph zine: two spot inks (federal blue and orange) on cream stock, overprint where they cross, a hard misregistered second pass on titles, halftone dots.

Risograph printing as used by independent publishers and zine makers (Riso Kagaku duplicators; studios such as Colorama and Hato Press).
