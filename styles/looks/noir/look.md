# Look: Noir (`noir`, the original)

The template's original look: a near-black gradient with purple and teal glows, a starfield, Space Grotesk, gradient
headline text, pills and glass. It isn't a `look.css`. It's what the template renders with no look applied, kept so
existing talks stay as they are. `tools/lint_look.mjs` flags 7 generic tells in it. Prefer a real look for new talks.
