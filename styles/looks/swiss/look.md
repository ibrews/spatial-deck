# Look: Swiss (`swiss`)

International Typographic Style: white paper, black grotesk, one signal red, flush-left on a six-column grid, hierarchy from size and position only. Use it for talks with one argument and hard numbers: research results, product launches, design-systems talks. Anywhere clarity is the point.

**Lineage.** Josef Müller-Brockmann, Grid Systems in Graphic Design (1981); Armin Hofmann and the Basel school posters; Neue Grafik (1958-65).

| Builder piece | In this look |
|---|---|
| Cover | Flush left on the grid. Event in red, the title very large and tight, the subtitle as a second size of the same face |
| Chapter | A giant red section numeral in the first column, title and lead beside it |
| Two-pane case | Plain image block, em-dash bullets |
| Statement | The largest type on any slide; no box, no rule |
| Full bleed / grid | 4px gutters, labels in the cell corner |
| Close | Same left margin as the cover; contacts under a hairline |
| Motion graphics | Ink on white with red as the accent; glow removed, screen blending becomes multiply |

**Motion grammar: cut.** Elements appear in two steps, .32 s, reading order with a 160 ms stagger. Reduced motion shows the final frame.

**Frame.** A 3px rule across the top with the project left and page right.

**Do** keep everything flush left and let size do the work. **Don't** center anything, add a second accent, or use a box where a rule will do.
