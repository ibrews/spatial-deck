# Look: Atlas (`atlas`)

A survey map: buff paper, a neatline with a map collar, brown contours, blue water, letterspaced caps for names and italic for water features. Use it for talks about place, sites, cities, travel, field work, data across geography, or a talk that is literally a map of a field.

**Lineage.** USGS 7.5-minute topographic quadrangle conventions (neatline, collar, scale bar, benchmarks); Swiss and British survey cartography.

| Builder piece | In this look |
|---|---|
| Cover | Spaced caps title, XR in water blue, italic subtitle in blue like a river name |
| Chapter | A benchmark triangle and "BM N", contour-brown kicker, a scale-bar rule |
| Two-pane case | Contour-ring bullets, italic blue subtitle |
| Statement | Spaced caps with an italic caption |
| Full bleed / grid | Neatline frames |
| Close | Same margin as the cover |
| Motion graphics | Ink on buff: teal to water blue, purple to woodland green, amber to contour brown |

**Motion grammar: survey.** Titles are revealed top to bottom like a sheet coming off the plotter, .7 s; copy fades in after. Reduced motion shows the final frame.

**Frame.** A map collar under the neatline: project left, presenter and event, scale bar and page right.

**Do** keep the contours faint and the type spaced. **Don't** fill areas with color; a survey map is line work.
