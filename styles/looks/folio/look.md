# Look: Folio (`folio`)

Editorial print on a black page. Warm white optical-size serif (Newsreader), one vermilion spot color, a running
head with the talk title and folio number, lowercase italic kickers ending in an ellipsis, and a large italic
numeral on chapter openers. Use it for narrative, essay-like and career-story talks, where the words carry the
slide.

**Lineage.** Magazine and book typography: running heads, folios, pull quotes with a hanging rule, plate captions.
It also descends directly from Alex's own NXT BLD 2020/2021 decks: serif titles on pure black and lowercase
"recently…" section openers (KB `projects/nxtbld/deck/anchored/style-analysis.md`). Returning audiences will
recognize it as his.

| Builder piece | In this look |
|---|---|
| Cover | Masthead between two rules, the title in roman with `.xr` in italic vermilion, subtitle in italic |
| Chapter | Large italic vermilion numeral, a lowercase italic kicker ("the advice…"), the title, then the lead under a hairline |
| Two-pane case | The image as a plate, bullets numbered with old-style italic figures |
| Statement | Pull quote with a 3px vermilion rule hanging at the left |
| Full bleed / grid | Captions as an italic line under the image, not floating chips |
| Close | Large roman title, italic sub, contact as a ruled line |
| Motion graphics | Warm white, gray and vermilion. Glow removed. Fonts switch to Newsreader |

**Motion grammar: set.** Type settles 0.35em upward and fades in reading order (0.8s, 220ms stagger).

**Do** let the words do the work, with one spot color and real italics. **Don't** add a second accent, or bold the
serif to make it "pop".
