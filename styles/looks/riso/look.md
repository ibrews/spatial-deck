# Look: Riso (`riso`)

A risograph zine: two spot inks (federal blue and orange) on cream stock, overprint where they cross, a hard misregistered second pass on titles, halftone dots. Use it for community, education, maker and culture talks; workshops; anything that should feel handmade and warm rather than corporate.

**Lineage.** Risograph printing as used by independent publishers and zine makers (Riso Kagaku duplicators; studios such as Colorama and Hato Press).

| Builder piece | In this look |
|---|---|
| Cover | The event on a tilted blue label, the title with an orange misregistered pass, the speaker on an orange overprint block |
| Chapter | A halftone disc in orange to the right, the number in a solid orange circle |
| Two-pane case | Orange dot bullets, image multiplied into the paper |
| Statement | Large blue type with the orange pass |
| Full bleed / grid | Images multiplied, orange labels |
| Close | Blue outline contacts |
| Motion graphics | Blue and orange on cream; glow removed, screen becomes multiply |

**Motion grammar: pass.** The second ink lands; elements drop from a 6px offset into register, .45 s with a slight overshoot. Reduced motion shows the final frame.

**Frame.** A page number at the bottom center, like a stapled zine.

**Do** use exactly two inks and let them overprint. **Don't** add a third color or any blur; misregistration is a hard offset, never a shadow.
