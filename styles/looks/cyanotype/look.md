# Look: Cyanotype (`cyanotype`)

Photogram plates: a Prussian blue ground, white where light was held back, plates numbered and captioned in italic. Use it for science, nature, history-of-technology and research talks; anything about process, patterns or specimens.

**Lineage.** Anna Atkins, Photographs of British Algae: Cyanotype Impressions (1843); the blueprint copies of engineering drawings that used the same process.

| Builder piece | In this look |
|---|---|
| Cover | Italic serif title, XR in pale paper tone, speaker between two hairlines |
| Chapter | "Plate N of M" kicker, italic title |
| Two-pane case | The photo printed as a cyanotype (grayscale screened into blue), roman-numeral captions |
| Statement | Italic serif |
| Full bleed / grid | Cells exposed in blue with italic captions |
| Close | Hairline contacts |
| Motion graphics | White on Prussian blue; glow removed |

**Motion grammar: expose.** Type develops in slowly, 1.2 s, 300 ms stagger. Reduced motion shows the final frame.

**Frame.** "Plate N / M" in italic, top right, inside a hairline border.

**Do** treat every image as a plate with a caption. **Don't** add a second hue; the process has one.
