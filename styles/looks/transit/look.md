# Look: Transit (`transit`)

Wayfinding signage: white grotesk on black sign panels, a white rule under the band, chapters as colored route bullets. Use it for talks that are a journey or a system: roadmaps, onboarding, architecture overviews, "how we get from here to there".

**Lineage.** NYCTA Graphics Standards Manual (Unimark International: Massimo Vignelli and Bob Noorda, 1970); airport and rail signage systems.

| Builder piece | In this look |
|---|---|
| Cover | Flush left on black, the XR word in signal yellow, speaker with a red line bar |
| Chapter | The section number in a route bullet; colors cycle red, green, yellow, blue |
| Two-pane case | Arrow bullets, yellow kicker |
| Statement | Large white type, yellow eyebrow |
| Full bleed / grid | Captions on black panels |
| Close | Contacts as sign panels with a green bar |
| Motion graphics | White on black, yellow and red as the accents; glow removed |

**Motion grammar: arrive.** Elements slide in .8em from the right and stop, .5 s, 140 ms stagger. Reduced motion shows the final frame.

**Frame.** A black sign band across the top with a white rule under it: project, presenter, page.

**Do** use one bullet color per chapter and keep copy short, the way a sign would. **Don't** mix more than one line color on a slide, or set long paragraphs.
