# Look: House (`house`)

A theater program. A warm black house and cream type, with a marquee-gold masthead band across the top (project,
presenter and event, page). Display type is condensed wood-type caps (Big Shoulders Display) and program copy is a
serif. Chapter cards sit in one hard-edged followspot circle, and the close is a curtain call with red curtain legs.
Use it for talks about live performance, events, hospitality, or anything that should feel like an evening, not a
pitch.

**Lineage.** Playbill covers (the masthead band), theater posters and billing blocks (stacked condensed caps), ticket
stubs, and curtain calls. The followspot is a lighting instrument with a hard edge, not a soft glow.

| Builder piece | In this look |
|---|---|
| Cover | Billing block: event in gold caps, a huge stacked title (`.xr` in gold), the speaker between gold rules |
| Chapter | Ticket-stub kicker ("No. 1 of 8"), the title in the followspot, a short gold bar |
| Two-pane case | The image in a thin gold outline, bullets as gold diamonds |
| Statement | A marquee line in condensed caps |
| Full bleed / grid | Labels as gold tags |
| Close | Curtain call: red curtain legs at both edges |
| Motion graphics | Gold, curtain red and cream on the house black. Glow removed. Fonts switch to Big Shoulders |

**Motion grammar: cue.** A lighting cue: dark, then up (0.42s ease-out). Supporting copy comes in on the next cue,
500ms later. Nothing slides.

**Do** keep the gold for the masthead and one accent per slide. **Don't** add more spotlights or soft glows, or
use red as text color.
